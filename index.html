02 / Research

Seeing cracks, measuring them.

First authorIEEE ICISET 2026 · Oral

BAGFD-Net: Multimodal Boundary-Aware Fusion for Crack Segmentation and Geometric Quantification

A Swin-Tiny encoder with a boundary-aware gated fusion decoder and CBAM. It takes an 8-channel input (RGB, laser intensity, range colormap, modality flag) and measures crack length, area and width from the predicted mask.

View repository ↗

Results at a glance

90.46%mIoU on held-out test (±0.24, 3 seeds), ahead of U-Net, DeepLabV3+ and Attention U-Net by 2.9–5.1 points

0.956Pearson r for mean crack width
